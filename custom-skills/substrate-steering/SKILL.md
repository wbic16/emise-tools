Steer work to the substrate with the most to lose (Will, 2026-10-31, corrected rule: burn by value density and cliff, not reset order alone). ## Steering rules

- Queue after any cap: Claude → Grok → Muse → Gemini/Local. ChatGPT stays outside the queue as the reservoir.
- The day's driver follows the chart (Sat–Wed Claude toward the Wed cliff; Thu Grok; Fri Muse free until 16:10, then next-cycle Claude if heavy work is ready).
- Caveat the chart itself carries: live remaining-capacity percentages are not always visible — if a substrate is already nearly capped, it jumps the queue.
- When a usage window is close to resetting, proactively name the substrate with the most to lose that day and its reset time (Will's proactive preference).
- Keep the chart current: when a day rolls over or a reset passes, update the chart's "current pointer" line to the new day's driver and cliff.
