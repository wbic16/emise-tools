# Opportunity identification — surface the window before it opens

Standing rule (Will, 2026-11-12): **identify opportunities as they are about to open.** Emise watches for competitions, calls, grants, and submission windows BEFORE they open or at opening, and surfaces entry prerequisites (identity verification, rule acceptance, account setup, eligibility) at identification time — an opportunity first surfaced near its deadline is a process failure.

Born of the Gemma 4 Paper Track (2026-11-12): found late in its window, draft ready on deadline morning, Kaggle identity verification still between Will and Submit — Will closed it rather than scramble.

## The practice

1. **Watch ahead of the window.** Track competitions, calls, grants, and submission windows whose opening is upcoming. Identification happens before opening or at opening — never first at the deadline.
2. **Surface prerequisites at identification time.** The moment an opportunity is identified, name what stands between Will and Submit: identity verification, rule acceptance, account setup, eligibility conditions, team/entry caps. Prerequisites with their own lead times (identity verification is the recorded example) get started, or explicitly queued for Will, at identification — not discovered on deadline morning.
3. **Create the lead time.** Record the opportunity with its window, deadline, and prerequisites where the work is tracked, so the entry can be prepared inside the window instead of against its edge.
4. **No last-second submissions.** If lead time was not created, the opportunity is reported as **missed-by-design**, not scrambled for. A scramble hides the process failure that caused it; the honest report is what fixes the watching.

## The publicity hold does not park identification

Submissions and publicity are held (Will, 2026-11-14): "let's hold off on submissions like this until our own roadmap and tooling are mature" — competition submissions (the Kaggle Gemma main track included) and public launches alike, until we're obviously ahead of the pack. **The hold parks submissions, not identification.** Watching, identifying, and surfacing prerequisites continue under the hold, so that when Will lifts it the lead time already exists.
