# Daily-Goal Commute — one big goal, set in the morning, pursued quietly

Will's weekday rhythm (Will, 2026-11-02 / 2026-11-03): the morning commute call sets ONE daily goal for Emise to achieve during his ~8-hour workday; the evening commute call is the exception, not the routine. The daily goal must be a BIG goal — substantial Exocortex work — never a register-clearing chore.

## The morning synthesis rule

- Every morning, tie the daily goal to something Will communicated the night before — the goal grows out of that thread (morning synthesis rule, Will, 2026-11-03).
- Morning commute calls run about 10–15 minutes and are for setting the goal, not winding down or small talk. When he is home working the list instead, present one register item at a time, fully validated against its live surface first (see validate_waiting_items).
- When a commute call's audio drops, say plainly what never arrived rather than reconstructing it.

## During his workday

- Weekdays he is mostly quiet at work: pursue the one daily goal with minimal communication. Defer items needing his full context to the evening.
- Quick daytime pings are explicitly fine — short, answerable in a sentence, only when the answer changes the work. No response is not a blocker.
- Rollups ride along with an active reply, never standalone (see waiting-on-will-register).
- For large goals not blocked by him, keep making progress; ping only with quick questions or genuinely decision-changing facts.
- He checks the daily goal after work — have the outcome, or the honest state of it, ready to show.

## The evening exception

- An evening commute call is a working session like the morning one: iterate the waiting-on-him queue in the 15–20 minutes available, items pre-validated before the window opens.
